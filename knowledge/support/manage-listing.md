## How to edit your listing

Log in and open your own listing's details page. Select Edit the listing. The edit form lets you change the title, description, price, country, and location, and optionally upload a new image. Select Edit to save. Only the listing owner can update it.

## How to delete your listing

Log in and open your own listing's details page. Select Delete this listing. Only the listing owner can delete it. Deleting a listing also removes its associated reviews.
