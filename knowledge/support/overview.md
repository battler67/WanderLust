## What WanderLust is

WanderLust is a portal for browsing accommodation listings created by users. Visitors can explore listings, read descriptions and reviews, and view a listing's location on a map. The portal currently does not provide a booking or payment flow.

## How to explore the portal

Open Explore to see listings. Use the category icons to browse types such as Rooms, Mountains, Pools, Camps, and Farms. The search box searches listing titles. Select a listing card to open its details page, where you can read its description, price, location, category, reviews, and map.
